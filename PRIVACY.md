## 2. Summary

- What your devices send to WireTrace stays on your computer. WireTrace never sends it anywhere.
- This website uses no cookies, no advertising and no cross-site tracking.

- Usage statistics are off unless you switch them on, and switching them off deletes them.
- We do not store IP addresses, and we never sell personal data or use it for advertising or profiling.

## 3. Information we collect

**When you visit this website.** Our hosting providers receive your IP address and browser details to deliver each page, as every website does. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie. It records the page visited, the referring page, your country, your browser, operating system and device type, and page load time. We see totals only.

**WireTrace: the update check.** While the update check is on, WireTrace contacts our update server when it starts and every 12 hours to find out whether a new version is available. Each check states the WireTrace version, operating system, processor type, how WireTrace was installed, and the date of its previous check. When WireTrace's version has changed since its previous check, by an update or by a return to an earlier version, its checks also state the version it changed from, with a random number used only to count that change once, until one of them is counted. If WireTrace did not record the version of an earlier check, as WireTrace 2.1 and earlier do not, they also state the date of the last such check. A check contains no identifier and no information about you, your devices or your data.

From the IP address of a check, our server works out your country and an approximate city. The IP address is used only briefly, to protect the service against abuse, and is never stored. We keep daily totals of checks and a short record of each version change; no other record of a single check is kept. You can turn the update check off in the Windows installer, or at any time in WireTrace's Preferences.

**WireTrace: usage statistics (optional).** Usage statistics are off by default, and the installer does not switch them on. WireTrace asks you once whether you want to share them, and you can change your answer at any time in Preferences.

If you switch them on, WireTrace creates a random identifier on your computer, not derived from your computer, your name or your network, and sends one report for each day it is used. A report states: the identifier and the date; the WireTrace version, operating system, processor type and how WireTrace was installed; which features were used; whether lines were lost, a log could not be written, a connection was lost or WireTrace closed unexpectedly; the highest line rate, the number of connections opened, the longest time one window stayed open and how many times WireTrace was started, each as a range; the theme; and the display scale.

A report never contains port or device names, file names, commands, or anything your devices sent. We store reports under a scrambled form of the identifier that cannot be turned back into it, and never with your IP address. When you switch usage statistics off, WireTrace deletes the identifier and everything it kept for them on your computer, and asks our server to delete every report sent under it.

**WireTrace: the diagnostic log.** WireTrace keeps a log of its own events on your computer, such as starting, ports opened and closed, errors and updates, to help find the cause of a problem. It never contains anything your devices sent or anything you typed, and it is never sent to us. It takes at most 5 MB, removing its oldest files. You can open it, or copy a summary of it to include in a report, from WireTrace's Help menu; it reaches us only if you send it yourself.

**When you contact us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains, and use it only to reply.

## 4. What we publish

The WireTrace usage page shows totals only: the number of active installations over recent periods, the countries and cities they are in, the versions and operating systems in use, update checks from WireTrace 1.x, and the number of downloads. No figure identifies an installation or a person, and a map marker sits on the city of a check, rounded to about 11 km, never on the location of a computer.

## 5. How we use information, and our legal basis

- **Delivering and securing this website, and counting visits:** our legitimate interest in operating and improving the website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **The update check:** offering updates, and understanding which versions and systems are in use and where; our legitimate interest in keeping WireTrace secure and up to date (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Usage statistics:** improving WireTrace; your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Section 6), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Replying to your email:** our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

You may object to processing based on legitimate interest, for example by blocking analytics in your browser or turning the update check off. We make no automated decisions about anyone.

## 7. International transfers

Our service providers process data in the countries where they operate, including the United States. They protect transfers out of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and India with recognised safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in their own policies.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **IP addresses:** not stored by us.
- **Update checks, as daily totals by country and city:** 2 years; after that only totals without the city remain.
- **Records of version changes** (the version changed from and to, their dates and the random number): 2 years.
- **Usage statistics reports:** 14 months; after that only totals that identify no installation remain.
- **Reports you ask us to delete, by switching usage statistics off:** deleted when the request arrives, and from all backups within 30 days.
- **Email:** for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep a record of our correspondence.

## 9. Security

We protect information with industry-standard safeguards: data is encrypted in transit, access to our figures is restricted to us, and identifiers are stored only in scrambled form. WireTrace installs only updates that are verified by digital signature.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access your personal data and receive a copy; have it corrected or deleted; restrict or object to its use; withdraw consent at any time; nominate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf; and have a grievance addressed by us.

To exercise a right, email [shahin@thenuanceproject.com](mailto:shahin@thenuanceproject.com). We reply within one month. Usage statistics are linked only to an identifier on your own computer, so we cannot find them from your name or email; switching usage statistics off in WireTrace deletes them, and we will help if you need it.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the authority in your country; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in India, the Data Protection Board of India.

## 11. Children

This website and WireTrace are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
